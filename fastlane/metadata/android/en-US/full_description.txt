Mystic Compass is a fully offline traditional-culture entertainment app.

Features:
• Locally calculated Chinese almanac information
• Entertainment-oriented six-line I Ching casting using a traditional probability model
• Simplified Sun, Moon, and Rising signs using birth date, birth time, city coordinates, and the city's historical IANA timezone

Privacy:
• No ads, analytics, tracking, accounts, cloud APIs, or device location
• Birth details are not saved by default
• Optional local saving requires explicit consent and can be cleared at any time
• The F-Droid Android build does not request Internet permission

This app is for cultural entertainment and self-reflection only. It is not medical, legal, financial, psychological, or other professional advice and does not provide deterministic predictions.
