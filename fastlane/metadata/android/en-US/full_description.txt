<b>Language notice</b>
The app interface and all divination content are currently available in Chinese only.

Fortune Telling blends Western astrology and traditional Chinese divination into a single, calm, offline companion.

<b>Western astrology</b>
Enter a birth date, time, and place to calculate your Sun, Moon, and Ascendant (rising) sign using real astronomical position data (via the astronomy-engine library), not a simplified date lookup table. Birth time and location are used only for the calculation on your device and are never sent anywhere.

<b>I Ching (Yi Jing) divination</b>
Cast one of the 64 hexagrams with a traditional coin-toss style ritual, including changing lines and the resulting transformed hexagram, each paired with interpretive guidance.

<b>Daily Chinese almanac (黄历)</b>
A daily card showing the lunar date, the day's guardian deity, auspicious and inauspicious activities (宜/忌), directional conflicts (冲煞), lunar mansion (星宿), and a reference table of auspicious hours (吉时参考), computed locally from the lunar calendar.

<b>Privacy</b>
The app makes no network requests and includes no analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind. All calculations run entirely on your device.

<b>Appearance</b>
Two visual themes are included, switchable at any time.
